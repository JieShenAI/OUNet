# OUNet image generation prompts
Mode: built-in image_gen (not CLI). Original PNG alpha is preserved.

## mobile

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production Android launcher icon master for OUNet, a personal Ocean University of China campus network auto-login utility, also basis for its desktop brand.
Primary request: Create one polished, original, minimal app icon: a bold white open circular O whose lower sweep becomes a single flowing ocean wave, with a small clear connection gap at upper right. Integrate ocean and reliable network connection as ONE memorable geometric symbol, not separate pictograms. Strong silhouette, beautifully balanced negative space, optically centered, legible at 24 pixels.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed solid ocean blue square background using the existing app color #004098. White symbol with one restrained existing cyan #60CEFF accent on the rising wave tip. Flat clean geometric graphic, crisp smooth edges.
Composition/framing: square 1:1 image, background extends to every edge, NO rounded corners on background (Android will apply its own mask). Main symbol entirely within central 60% width and height to survive circular and squircle launcher masks. No border or outer padding outside the blue square.
Constraints: only one icon, no text, no letters except the abstract O shape, no school crest, no wordmark, no presentation sheet, no device mockup, no shadow, no 3D, no glass, no texture, no gradient, no fine details, no watermark.

## desktop

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Windows desktop application icon for OUNet.
Input images: Image 1 is the approved OUNet mobile app icon and is the exact brand reference.
Primary request: Adapt the same icon to Windows desktop: preserve the exact white open O / ocean wave symbol, the cyan wave tip, proportions, colors and flat graphic style. Place the symbol on a solid #004098 rounded square tile with smooth corner radius approximately 22% of tile side. Center the tile on a square canvas and leave about 7% transparent margin around it. Keep the symbol clearly readable at small taskbar sizes.
Constraints: background OUTSIDE the rounded tile must be genuinely transparent alpha, blue tile interior fully opaque. One icon only, no text, no added elements, no mockup, no shadow, no glow, no bevel, no 3D. Preserve the distinctive original symbol geometry and palette.

## logo

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: transparent horizontal primary logo for OUNet, a personal campus network connection utility.
Input images: Image 1 is the OUNet mobile icon; use as strict brand reference for the original circular open O / wave symbol.
Primary request: Create one clean horizontal logo lockup on genuinely transparent background. On the left use the same original open O and wave geometry, recolored ocean blue #004098 with its cyan #60CEFF wave tip. On the right put the exact wordmark "OUNet" in a refined modern geometric sans serif, bold but friendly, ocean blue #004098, clearly readable, impeccable spacing. The N is uppercase and e,t lowercase: O U N e t. The symbol and wordmark are visually balanced. No blue square behind the mark for this logo; free-standing blue mark.
Composition/framing: wide horizontal canvas about 3:1 ratio; generous clear margins; symbol about the cap height plus 25%; wordmark and symbol centered vertically, sensible gap.
Constraints: exactly one logo, verbatim text "OUNet", no other text, no slogans, no school crest, no background color, no presentation board, no mockups, no shadows, no textures, no 3D, crisp flat vector-like edges. Preserve brand symbol silhouette from reference.
